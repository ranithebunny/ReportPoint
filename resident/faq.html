FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a report take to become resolved?
Resolution times depend on the category and severity of the issue. Routine maintenance concerns are typically reviewed within 24 to 48 hours, while urgent safety or utility issues are prioritized immediately.
What do I do if my report is rejected?
Open your report on the Track page to view the administrator’s rejection reason and notes. You can submit a new report with clearer details, exact location information, or supporting photo/video evidence.
Who do I contact if I have further questions about my report?
You can reply directly through your report’s thread on the Track page or call the College Point front desk at +63 954 488 7599 (Globe) or +63 962 626 7420 (Smart) with your tracking number.
What do I do if my report has not been updated in a while?
Check the activity timeline on the Track page to see if administrators requested additional information. If no update appears after 3 business days, reach out to the admin office using your tracking code.
Are these reports documented by the administrators of CollegePoint?
Yes. Every submitted report is logged with a unique tracking number and timestamped status history so administrators can track repairs, follow-ups, and building maintenance records.
Who can see my report?
Only authorized College Point administrators and you (using your tracking number or resident account) can view the details of your report. Personal contact details are kept confidential.
